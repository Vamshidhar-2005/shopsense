Advanced Feature 1: ML ARIMA Time-Series Demand Forecasting

📐 ARIMA(1,1,1) Equation

y_t = y_{t-1} + φ_1 * (y_{t-1} - μ) + θ_1 * ε_{t-1} + ε_t Where: • φ_1: Auto-Regressive (AR) lag coefficient • θ_1: Moving Average (MA) residual coefficient • μ: Mean differenced daily sales velocity

📈 Forecasting Outputs

  • Daily Velocity: Units sold per day over 30/60/90 days.
  • Stockout Risk Days: Estimated days remaining until stock reaches zero.
  • Safety Restock Qty: Recommended reorder quantity with 7-day safety buffer.
🗣️ Speaker Script
"Advanced Feature 1 trains an ARIMA(1,1,1) time-series model. By calculating auto-regressive lag and moving average parameters on historical daily sales, it predicts future demand and reorder quantities."

Advanced Feature 3: Vector Search & Semantic Recommendations

📐 Vector Cosine Similarity

Similarity(A, B) = (A • B) / (||A|| * ||B||) Where A = Query Embedding Vector (16-D) B = Product Embedding Vector (16-D)

🔍 Match Confidence Output

  • Converts catalog descriptions into 16-dimensional dense vector embeddings.
  • Ranks products by Cosine Similarity match percentage (e.g. 🎯 100% Match).

Faculty Q&A & Project Conclusion

❓ Prepared Q&A Answers

  • Q: Database failure handling? Answer: Auto self-healing fallback to local SQLite database (shopsense_fallback.db).
  • Q: Why ARIMA over linear regression? Answer: ARIMA(1,1,1) accounts for autocorrelation and past forecast errors.
  • Q: Deduplication of products? Answer: Concept-level normalization updates existing items instead of inserting duplicate rows.
